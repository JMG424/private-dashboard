Operating distinction

Chatbot vs agent

A chatbot completes one conversational turn. An agent receives a goal, loads its operating rules, plans, uses allowed tools, pauses for approval, acts, and returns a receipt.

Conversation model

Prompt → answer → idle

Helpful in the current exchange, but it does not own the follow-through.

01

Intake

Capture the desired outcome, deadline, constraints, and decision owner.

02

Route

The coordinator assigns the smallest qualified specialist set.

03

Prepare

Specialists research, draft, check, or structure the work.

04

Approve

05
